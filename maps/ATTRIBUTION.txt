MAP DATA ATTRIBUTION
====================

Rosebridge map series — "Rosebridge" (Copenhagen and surrounding areas)

  rosebridge.jpg           full-detail master (11776×11264, source for the rest)
  rosebridge_overview.jpg  moderate-quality whole map (menu + overview mode)
  rosebridge_menu.jpg      menu grid image
  sectors/rosebridge_*.jpg 9 native-resolution sector crops (a1 … c3)

Rendering: fully custom vector artwork — pale road network on a muted
teal-green "fabric" base, inspired by open-world game atlas styles.
No labels, no addresses, no satellite imagery in the final artwork.

Source data: © OpenStreetMap contributors — www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
(roads, railways, waterways, parks and lakes licensed under ODbL).
Sea / Øresund geometry derived once from Esri World Imagery basemap tiles
(Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community) © Esri and its
licensors — www.esri.com — used only as a shoreline mask, not visible imagery.

Rendered as a static one-off composite for personal roleplay use.

NEXA emblem © the respective owners; uploaded by the app operator.
